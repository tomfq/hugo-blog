# About this unit

Have you ever had a pet, or made friends with someone else’s pet? Pets can help people who are lonely or stressed. Different kinds of pets have different qualities, like size and personality. In this unit, we’ll be learning more about different kinds of pets and how we can help a person to choose a type of pet.
